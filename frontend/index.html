How it works

The same assistant handles every conversation, whether it starts on your website or on WhatsApp.

1

Customers message you

Through your website chat widget or WhatsApp, where enabled.

2

Jantar AI answers instantly

From your own menu, opening hours, location, and FAQs.

3

Bookings happen automatically

Features

Everything below is part of the platform today.

AI website chat

A conversational assistant for your website, powered by AI.

Menu, hours & FAQ answering

Answers are generated from your restaurant's own information.

Embeddable chat widget

A branded chat widget you can add to your own website.

Table booking with capacity checks

Bookings are checked against your restaurant's seating capacity automatically.

Booking confirmation email

Customers can receive an automatic email confirming their booking.

WhatsApp channel

Handle the same conversations and bookings over WhatsApp.

Admin dashboard

Manage your restaurant's profile, menu, hours, FAQs, and bookings.

Conversation history

Review past conversations between your customers and the assistant.
