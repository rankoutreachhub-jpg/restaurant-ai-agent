1. Service overview

Jantar AI ("the Service") provides restaurants with an AI-powered chat assistant, table booking, and related admin tools. Today the Service includes: AI website chat (answering from a restaurant's own menu, opening hours, location, and FAQ information), an embeddable website chat widget, WhatsApp messaging (where a restaurant has it configured), table booking with automatic capacity checks, optional booking-confirmation emails, and a restaurant admin dashboard. Some capabilities described on the Pricing page are marked "Coming soon" — these are not yet part of the Service and are not covered by these terms until they are actually released.

2. Restaurant responsibilities

A restaurant using the Service is responsible for:

  • The accuracy of the menu, opening hours, location, FAQ answers, and other information it enters, since the AI assistant answers customers directly from this data.
  • Honoring bookings made through the Service, in the same way it would honor a booking taken by phone or in person.
  • Keeping its admin API key(s) confidential, and letting us know if a key may have been exposed so it can be rotated.
  • Its own additional legal or privacy obligations toward its customers, which this Service does not replace (see also our Privacy Policy).

3. Customer responsibilities

4. Account and admin access

There is currently no public self-service sign-up for the admin dashboard. Restaurant admin access is granted directly by the platform operator as a long-lived API key, scoped to one or more specific restaurants. A key can be rotated (invalidating the old one immediately) or deactivated on request. You are responsible for anything done using your key while it is active and not reported as compromised.

5. Bookings and restaurant-provided information

Bookings can be made through the website chat, the embeddable widget, WhatsApp (where enabled), or directly by a restaurant's own admin team. Every booking is checked against that restaurant's configured seating capacity for the requested date and time before it is accepted. Jantar AI is not a party to the booking itself — it is an arrangement between the customer and the restaurant — and the AI assistant's replies are generated from whatever menu, hours, and FAQ information the restaurant has entered. We do not independently verify the accuracy of restaurant-provided information.
